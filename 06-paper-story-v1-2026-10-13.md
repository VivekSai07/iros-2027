# Paper story (working draft — H1-benchmark+method)

**Working title:** "Say It Mid-Motion: Benchmarking and Repairing Spoken Interruptions During Manipulation on a Low-Cost Arm"

## Narrative
1. People correct collaborators *while* they act ("not that one — the left bowl").
2. Current systems stop (ELLSA), retract (VITA-E), wait for step boundaries (Hi Robot), or only act early on partial speech (Premover, RIAP). None measure or optimise what happens *physically* when speech overlaps motion.
3. We contribute (a) a benchmark with a physical failure taxonomy and time-anchored metrics, (b) a low-cost mid-motion repair method, (c) evidence that the benchmark predicts human-rated fluency.

## Contributions
- C1: Interruption-in-manipulation benchmark (sim + real SO-101), failure taxonomy, metrics (repair latency, trajectory continuity, post-repair success, false-barge-in rate, retraction penalty, residual motion).
- C2: Mid-motion repair via chunk truncation + inpainting-style continuation (RTC) on SmolVLA/ACT with a streaming ASR + turn/backchannel + small-LLM cascade; runs on commodity GPUs.
- C3: Human study (n≈18–39, within-subject) showing correlation of benchmark metrics with Hoffman fluency metrics and PMQ/SUS.

## Research questions
- RQ1: What are the failure modes of current stop/retract/wait strategies under spoken overlap?
- RQ2: Does mid-motion repair beat stop-and-replan on continuity and success?
- RQ3: Do benchmark metrics predict human-perceived fluency?

## Experiments
- Baselines: stop-and-replan (LeRobot async), step-boundary wait (Hi Robot-style), retract-and-restart (VITA-E-style), ELLSA only if code is released and feasible.
- Interruption types: target change, constraint addition, speed/manner, cancel, backchannel (should NOT interrupt), self-correction.
- Speech: voice-cloned TTS users in sim (EchoChain protocol) + real speakers.
- Real SO-101 pick-place scenes reuse the existing dataset/stack; extend with interruptions.
- Evaluation: success, latency, continuity, false barge-in, Hoffman idle/concurrent/functional delay, PMQ/SUS.

## Why it can stand out
Application-flavoured (real arm + live speech), reusable benchmark, extends across applications (kitchen, lab, workshop). Best-paper potential depends on the human-validation story and clean failure taxonomy.

## Risks and fallbacks
- Scoop (medium-high) → differentiate on physical taxonomy + validity study; fallback ICRA 2028.
- Marginal method gains → lean on benchmark + study (systematic-study framing).
- Ethics/participants → start approval route now.
