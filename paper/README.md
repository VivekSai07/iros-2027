# Paper And Submission

Manuscript source, citation database, reviewed figures, supplementary text, and submission checklists live here. Do not adopt a paper title or contribution before the team chooses the question. The existing [candidate story](../knowledge/06-paper-story.md) remains provisional.

Add the conference template and build instructions after verifying the official requirements. Generated builds belong in ignored `build/`. Link result claims to [run records](../results/README.md) and citations to primary-source notes. Before submission check authorship, anonymization, ethics, licenses, and required tool-use disclosures; the human team approves and submits.
