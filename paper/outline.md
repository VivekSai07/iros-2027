Insert the adopted question only after the M2 team decision. Keep alternatives and rejected questions in `knowledge/`, not in the manuscript narrative.

## 6. Results

## 7. Discussion, limitations, and ethics

Explain scope limits, counterexamples, safety constraints, participant protections, and what the evidence does not establish.
